Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions parents ask most.

Which grades do you teach?

We teach Maths for Grades 4 to 9, covering the Primary and Lower Secondary syllabus.

Is the first session really free?

Yes! The free trial lets your child meet the tutor, try the online classroom and receive a quick level assessment.

How long is each lesson?

Lessons are 60 minutes. Shorter or longer sessions can be arranged on request.

What platform do you use?

Live lessons run on Zoom or Google Meet with a shared digital whiteboard. A link is sent before each session.

Can my child join group classes?

We offer 1-to-1 and small-group lessons (max 4 students) so everyone gets personal attention.

What if we miss a session?

Please notify us at least 12 hours in advance and we will happily reschedule.

How will I know my child is improving?

You receive regular test results and a progress report, and can speak with the tutor any time.
